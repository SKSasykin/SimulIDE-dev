Ground-referenced sine-wave voltage rail.

AC Rail is always active and has one output referenced to the simulator ground.
The configured voltage is RMS. Peak voltage is Voltage RMS * sqrt(2).

Property: (default value)

- Voltage RMS: (220 V)
   Effective (RMS) output voltage.

- Frequency: (50 Hz)
   Sine-wave frequency.
   Maximum value is 10 GHz.
