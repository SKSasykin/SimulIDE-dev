MAX31855 Thermocouple Converter

The component emulates the MAX31855 read-only SPI interface. Connect CS, CLK,
and DO to the SPI master. CS is active low, and the 32-bit result is shifted
out MSB first in SPI mode 0.

In manual mode, Thermocouple Temp. defines the measured temperature. The
up/down controls change it by Temp. increment. Internal Temp. defines the cold
junction temperature included in the output frame.

Enable External thermocouple to expose the + and - inputs. In this mode the
manual temperature controls are hidden, and the measured temperature is
calculated from the differential input voltage using a type-K sensitivity of
41 uV/C plus Internal Temp. cold-junction compensation. Both inputs must be
connected; otherwise the OC and FAULT bits are asserted.

Grounded hides the GND pin and connects it internally. When Grounded is off,
the visible GND pin can be connected manually.

The Faults properties can force the datasheet OC, SCG, and SCV flags. Any
active fault also sets the common FAULT bit.
