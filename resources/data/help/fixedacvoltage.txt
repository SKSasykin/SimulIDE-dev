Switchable ground-referenced sine-wave voltage source.

Turn On/Off with the left button. When off, the output is held at 0 V.
The configured voltage is RMS. Peak voltage is Voltage RMS * sqrt(2).

Property: (default value)

- Voltage RMS: (220 V)
   Effective (RMS) output voltage.

- Frequency: (50 Hz)
   Sine-wave frequency.
   Maximum value is 10 GHz.

- Small size: (false)
   Use the compact component body.
