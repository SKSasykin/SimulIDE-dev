Dual Heater

This component models two electrically isolated heating elements embedded in one
metal body and a thermocouple located between them. All six terminals are
independent: H1+/H1-, TC+/TC-, and H2+/H2-.

The heater branches are resistors. Their actual heat is calculated from the
instantaneous terminal voltage, so AC, DC, phase control, and PWM supplies are
supported. Rated power, rated voltage, and resistance are linked by R=V^2/P.
Changing power or voltage recalculates resistance; changing resistance
recalculates rated power. Resistance is specified at 20 °C and rises with body
temperature according to the configurable wire temperature coefficient.

The body temperature follows C*dT/dt=P-G*(T-Tambient). Thermal capacity is
calculated from body dimensions and the selected material. Heat loss combines
air convection over the body surface and thermal radiation. Aluminum and air
are the initial material and environment choices.

Thermocouple lag is calculated from the heater-to-sensor distance and material
thermal diffusivity. The TC terminals provide a floating voltage equal to the
configured sensitivity multiplied by the sensor-to-ambient temperature
difference.

Double-click the component, or use Open temperature plot in its context menu,
to inspect body and thermocouple temperatures. The plot window duration is
editable, Follow latest keeps the view at the current simulation time, and the
horizontal scrollbar navigates retained history.
